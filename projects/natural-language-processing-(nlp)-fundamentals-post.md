## Core NLP Concepts

### Tokenization
Tokenization involves breaking down text into smaller units, such as words or phrases, to facilitate analysis. It is a fundamental step in NLP preprocessing tasks.

### Part-of-Speech Tagging
Part-of-Speech tagging assigns grammatical categories (like noun, verb, adjective) to words in a sentence, aiding in syntax analysis and language understanding.

### Named Entity Recognition
Named Entity Recognition identifies and classifies named entities in text, such as names of people, organizations, or locations, crucial for information extraction tasks.

### Sentiment Analysis
Sentiment Analysis determines the emotional tone of text, helping in understanding opinions, sentiments, and attitudes expressed in reviews, social media posts, and more.

## Recent Advancements in NLP
